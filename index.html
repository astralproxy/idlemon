How to Play

Idle Souls is a fan-made recreation of Dark Souls that plays itself. But, you are still an active participant and it is possible to die.

Choose an enemy from the target list. Your character attacks automatically once per game-second. If you choose a different enemy during a fight, you must finish the current fight before switching.

All Loot goes into your Bag. Equip an item to an available Equipment slot to activate its associated skill; equipping another item in that slot returns the previous item to your Bag.

Enemies deal their listed damage between player attacks. Your Armor reduces incoming damage by both a flat amount and/or a percent depending on the items equipped.

There are certain Items that heal you, but at zero health: YOU DIED, and the game over screen will appear. Special Items you collect may revive you though.

Only dying or defeating a Boss pauses combat. Simply choose a new enemy to resume. Manually switching to Prepare does not pause.

Combat also pauses while the Menu is open or while you are viewing a menu page like Profile or Settings

Progress is saved in this browser. Use the Profile page to copy a key to bring your progress to another machine.
