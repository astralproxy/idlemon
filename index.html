How to play

Help

Choose a monster from the target list. Your character attacks automatically once per game-second; change Game Speed in Settings to adjust how quickly game time passes. If you choose a different monster during a fight, your character finishes the current monster before switching. Monsters deal their listed damage between player attacks; armor reduces incoming damage.

Loot goes into your Bag. Equip an item to an available Equipment slot to activate its associated skill; equipping another item in that slot returns the previous item to your Bag. Damage starts at 1 and increases by the sum of the equipped items' values.

Use an item with the Flee skill to abandon the current monster and pause in Prepare. Choose a monster after returning to Fight to resume combat.

Skills activate only when their listed level is reached and their associated loot is equipped. Monster health and experience rewards are based on monster level.

Manually switching to Prepare or Shop does not pause combat. Defeating a Boss pauses combat and opens the Shop. Choose a different monster to resume.

At zero player health, combat pauses and a Game Over screen shows your total monsters defeated. A Revive skill item in your Bag can restore 100 health and continue the fight.

Combat also pauses while the main menu is open or while you are viewing Profile, Help, or another menu page.

Progress is saved in this browser. Use the Profile page to copy a restore key and bring a progress snapshot to another machine. Offline progress is capped at eight hours.
